The Killeen Healing Rooms

The Killeen Healing Rooms are open the second Saturday of each month from 9:30 – 11:30 am and the last Tuesday of the month from 6:45 – 8:00 pm, at the KPLE-TV building (502 E. Elms Rd., in Killeen). Our commission is based on Mark 16:17-18, “And these signs shall follow those that believe…they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover.” We will have prayer teams available to agree with you in prayer and see you healed in the Name of Jesus Christ.
