SECTION 1: ACCOUNT TIERS ON VIBE NETWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Vibe Network supports three primary participation tiers:

1. Viewer Account:
   - Designed for fans, shoppers, and community members.
   - Watch live broadcasts and on-demand videos, chat in real time, follow favorite creators, subscribe to premium channels, book 1-on-1 calls with creators, tip during streams, and shop official merchandise.

2. Creator / Influencer Channel:
   - A targeted, single-brand broadcasting hub built for an individual creator, coach, athlete, musician, pastor, or boutique brand.
   - Optimized for direct viewer intimacy, frictionless setup, live streaming, VOD publishing, community posts, store products, paid bookings, and monthly fan subscriptions.

3. Enterprise / White-Label Network (N2N Architecture):
   - A full multi-tenant digital media ecosystem designed for media companies, sports leagues, collegiate conferences, corporate parent brands, franchises, and entrepreneurs.
   - Powered by Vibe's proprietary Network-to-Network (N2N) architecture, allowing the network owner to host, syndicate, and monetize multiple child networks and creator channels under one umbrella.


SECTION 2: BENEFITS OF OWNING A CHANNEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct Relationship & Attention Capture: Connect with fans in a dedicated, distraction-free space with no competing recommendation sidebars or algorithm suppression.
- Monetization Flexibility: Combine monthly subscriptions, pay-per-view videos, live chat tipping, 1-on-1 consulting/coaching call bookings, and direct merchandise sales in one profile.
- Frictionless Customization: Customize cover art, avatars, bios, photo flipbooks, custom backgrounds, and broadcast schedules in minutes.
- Low Operational Overhead: Instant setup lets creators focus 100% of their time on content, community, and commerce.
- Optimal Use Cases: Individual content creators, fitness coaches and pro athletes, recording artists, educators, consultants, and niche e-commerce brands.


SECTION 3: BENEFITS OF OWNING A NETWORK (N2N ARCHITECTURE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Scale via Aggregation (The Platform Effect): Your network grows exponentially as hosted child channels and creators publish content and bring their own audiences—you are never bottlenecked by producing all content yourself.
- Enterprise Revenue Models: Earn configurable platform revenue shares across child channel transactions, offer SaaS/channel-builder tiers, sell network-wide sponsorships, and run unified or per-channel storefronts.
- Instant Multi-Tenant Brand Syndication: Spin up customized child networks in clicks—each with its own domain/subdomain, logo, accent colors, hero banner, store, and live feed—while retaining central administrative control.
- Centralized N2N Command Center: Manage your entire ecosystem from the Business Admin Dashboard, including:
  * Global Analytics & Live Telemetry
  * Hero & Global Branding Editors
  * Child Network Fleet Management (create, activate/deactivate, or edit child networks)
  * Network Users Directory & CRM Leads Inbox
  * Network Ledger & Creator Split Management
  * Child Branding Editor
- Enhanced Business Valuation: Diversified multi-creator media networks historically command significantly higher institutional valuation multiples (8x–15x+ annual EBITDA) compared to single-creator channels (1x–3x annual net earnings).
- Optimal Use Cases: Sports leagues and combat promotions, collegiate NIL collectives, music labels and artist groups, chambers of commerce, faith-based broadcast ministries, franchises, and media entrepreneurs.


SECTION 4: SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISON — CHANNEL VS. NETWORK (N2N)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Value Proposition:
   - Channel Model: Content expertise and direct viewer engagement.
   - Network Model (N2N): Distribution infrastructure and multi-brand ecosystem.

2. Primary Revenue Streams:
   - Channel Model: Fan tips, merchandise, 1-on-1 bookings,PPV videos, and monthly channel subscriptions.
   - Network Model (N2N): Platform revenue-share across child channels, network sponsorships/ads, enterprise merchandising, and SaaS/syndication fees.

3. Content Dependency:
   - Channel Model: High (relies on the creator's ongoing content output).
   - Network Model (N2N): Low (leverages the combined output of all hosted child channels and creators).

4. Audience Scaling:
   - Channel Model: Linear (grows through direct fan acquisition).
   - Network Model (N2N): Exponential (compound growth across every syndicated child channel).

5. Typical Asset Valuation Multiple:
   - Channel Model: 1x to 3x annual net earnings.
   - Network Model (N2N): 8x to 15x+ annual platform EBITDA.


SECTION 5: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS — CHANNELS VS. NETWORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Should I start with a Channel or a Network on Vibe?
A: If you are an individual creator, coach, or single brand building your initial audience, starting with a Creator Channel is the fastest and most capital-efficient way to launch and monetize immediately. If you manage multiple talent rosters, teams, schools, partner brands, or want to build a scalable media company where other creators broadcast under your umbrella, launching an N2N Network is the ideal choice. You can also start as a Channel and upgrade into a full Network as you grow.

Q: How do revenue splits work between a Parent Network and its Creators / Child Networks?
A: Network administrators can configure a default platform fee percentage for their network (commonly 15% to 30%, depending on the network agreement) as well as custom per-creator split percentages in the Monetization CRM / Wallet settings. Every transaction automatically calculates the net creator share and records entries in the Network Ledger.
